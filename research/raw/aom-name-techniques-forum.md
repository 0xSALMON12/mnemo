 Hi all,
I am a new user of the site and am trying hard to improve my memory for names. The tutorial is a great illustration of how the technique works and worked very well for me. I am sadly, pretty confounded by the training.

- The one-minute memorization time seems so short - I was lucky on a couple of attempts and now am trying to memorize 10 names in 60s or 6s per name. Most of the time I just can’t generate a memorable visual image for a name in 60s let alone 6s. How do you experts develop the facility to do this so quickly? I have downloaded screen snips of the faces and names from the name training. I then visited the “wordhippo rhyme” website looking for visualizable words for the names, and then learned them, in the hope that doing this multiple times might sharpen my ability. It has helped a bit, but generating images so fast just seems way out of my reach at the moment. Any tips on how to improve this?

- I am also puzzled by the 4-minute recall time, as I either remember or forget, but don’t seem to be able to improve recall by spending the other 3.5 minutes thinking about the lost names. Would you please explain why the recall time is longer in training? What do you do in the longer time to encourage the lost names to appear?

- I have made my own list of over 100 names with the associated hard-won images and memorized most of them. In the training, this helps a little, but not much, as the training list of names must be much longer - probably over 500 or 1000. There are people on the site who can remember 15 names in 60s. Do you just memorize a very long list of name images? How many?

- It seems so daunting. Can someone please give me a realistic road map of how to get sufficiently proficient to be able to remember multiple new names and faces in a short space of time?

 Welcome to the threads,

Just from one person’s perspective - I benefitted from making a list of 500 names (but later expanded it to include names that I missed in actual games/practice on the ML page). You can web-search “top-names” for men or women in the language of your choice.

Then, you should take the time to assign the first thing that comes to mind when you see each name, and write that association in a flashcard app (or however you like - Quizlet works well for me, and is free online). It’s important to rely on the first thing that “pops” into your head, no matter how strange or even if other’s wouldn’t know why that’s an association for you.

Simply running through the names when you have 10-15 min at a time, and soon you should see results in actual memorization practice. That’s just one approach, and there’s a lot of other great things in the “Resources” tab at the top of this page to help new folks too.

Best of luck!
